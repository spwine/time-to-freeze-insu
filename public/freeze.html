Methodology (from workbook)
The spreadsheet estimates convection, conduction, and radiation heat transfer for each pipe size. Convection is computed from Reynolds, Prandtl, and Nusselt number (then h). Conduction is modeled as series resistances through pipe wall, insulation, and cladding. Radiation uses emissivity ε and the Stefan–Boltzmann constant σ. [1]Freeze Protection Design Basis Best Practices Document No.: EDS-BP-0226
Workbook usage notes: analyze up to 14 standard pipes plus one custom pipe; stainless schedules supported are 5/10/40/80; if no insulation/cladding, use very small thickness (e.g., 0.0001 in). [1]Freeze Protection Design Basis Best Practices Document No.: EDS-BP-0226
